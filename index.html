Questions the book asks.

What happens to my entries if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. The book keeps working on the Starter limits: one book, three people, unlimited entries. The paid features simply stop, and you can export everything before or after.

Is any of this uploaded?

No. Every entry is written to this browser's local storage. That is why the free plan can exist: there is no server to pay for. Clearing site data clears the book, which is also the only way to lose it.

How do I get more people into one book?

The key is the invitation. Read the six digits out and anyone who types them opens the same list. The plan only sets how many people can hold that key at once.

Can two people be in the same entry?

An entry has one date, one type, one category and one amount. If two people paid, write two lines and a note on each; the balance adds them either way.

Do you refund a month I did not use?

Yes, within 14 days, no question asked. Cancel in the same place you subscribed and the book stays exactly where it is.
